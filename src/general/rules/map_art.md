## Overview
MUST integrate the `content_image` into the `map_image` in a harmonious, HOLD the full map lines, choose a most suitable area to integrate `content_image` under it. 

For Analyser:
Analysis the map image and content image, construct a **most harmonious way**(contours, shapes...) to integrated.

For Planner:
Overview are typical two steps, you can refer them and generated **detail commands on how/where** to integrated the two images.
1. First extract the sketch of content image.
2. Integrated the sketch with map image in a suitable plan.
